Methodology

Data Sources

  • Texas Railroad Commission — well data, drilling permits, production reports
  • County Tax Assessor — property tax records, mailing addresses, delinquent tax flags
  • County Probate Court — estate filings, will probates, heir identification
  • County Clerk — mineral deed recordings, assignments
  • Secretary of State — LLC, trust, partnership filings and registered agents
  • Public OSINT — property appraiser records, voter registration, corporate filings

Scoring Formula (1–100)

  • Royalty Estimate: 40 pts (thresholds at $100K, $250K, $500K)
  • Portfolio Complexity: 20 pts (single tract vs. multiple operators)
  • Trigger Events: 15 pts (permit, probate, tax distress)
  • Contactability: 15 pts (phone/email/mailing available)
  • Willingness Signal: 10 pts (estate, recent sale, etc.)
